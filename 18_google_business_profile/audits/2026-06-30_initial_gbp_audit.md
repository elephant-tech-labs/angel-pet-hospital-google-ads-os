# Initial Google Business Profile Audit - 2026-06-30

Auditor: ChatGPT strategy review from user-provided GBP screenshots.

Raw screenshot policy: do not commit raw screenshots to this public repository.

## Executive verdict

Angel Pet Hospital's Google Business Profile has real local demand, but the profile is under-optimized and weaker than nearby competitors in trust and conversion presentation.

Current status: average visibility, below-average trust compared with competitors, and incomplete conversion setup.

## Main findings

### Strengths

- GBP is already producing meaningful calls, directions, and website clicks.
- Primary category appears appropriate: Veterinarian.
- Additional category Animal hospital is reasonable.
- Phone number is consistent with the main number used in ads and website work: (425) 673-0700.
- Search discovery terms validate the need for General Vet, Urgent Vet, Animal Hospital, and Brand strategies.
- Hours have been confirmed by Guri as correct.
- Appointment URL has now been added.
- Description has been updated after the initial audit.

### Weaknesses

- Public profile rating is 3.8 with 213 reviews, which is weak versus local competitors.
- Competitors show stronger review profiles, often 4.6 to 4.9 ratings with more reviews.
- Posts are outdated, with examples from around 3 years ago.
- Photos/media setup appears incomplete; GBP is prompting for cover photo, logo, and business photos.
- Services list is messy and includes potentially inaccurate or risky items.
- No booking activity was shown in the captured performance screenshot before the appointment URL update.
- Google has auto-updated some fields, so the profile needs active monitoring.

## Review strategy

The profile needs a structured review recovery plan.

90-day target:
- 30-50 new quality reviews from real happy clients.

Best review ask sources:
- happy dental/spay/neuter clients
- successful urgent visits
- new puppy/kitten visits
- long-term clients
- clients who mention fair price or kind staff

Avoid asking after:
- billing disputes
- long waits
- complicated outcomes
- surprise pricing concerns
- unhappy urgent cases
