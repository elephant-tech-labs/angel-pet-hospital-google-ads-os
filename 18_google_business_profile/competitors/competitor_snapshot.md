### Trust gap

Angel's rating is materially weaker than most nearby general vet competitors.

Priority response:

### Booking gap

Competitors show visible Book online buttons.

Priority response:
- appointment URL has now been added

### Promotions opportunity

Some competitors use owner posts and offers. Angel now has a /promotions page and should publish GBP posts pointing to it.

### Emergency intent competition

For urgent/emergency searches, Angel competes against 24-hour emergency clinics. Angel should not claim 24/7 care if not true.

Recommended wording:
- Urgent Vet Care During Business Hours
- Call ahead during business hours
- If life-threatening or after-hours, contact a 24-hour emergency hospital
