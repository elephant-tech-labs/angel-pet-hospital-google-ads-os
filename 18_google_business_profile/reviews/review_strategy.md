# Google Business Profile Review Strategy

Initial review audit date: 2026-06-30

## Current issue

Public profile snapshot showed:
- Rating: 3.8
- Reviews: 213

This rating is a major conversion weakness versus local competitors that show stronger ratings and similar or higher review counts.

## Goal

Improve trust and conversion by increasing recent, legitimate, high-quality reviews.

90-day target:
- 30-50 new real reviews from happy clients
- Improve review recency and positive keyword coverage
- Reduce the visible impact of old/negative reviews

## Review themes to encourage naturally

Do not script fake reviews. Encourage clients to mention what they genuinely appreciated.

Useful themes:
- kind staff
- clear communication
- fair/reasonable pricing
- urgent or same-day help
- dental care
- spay/neuter care
- puppy/kitten care
- cat care
- bilingual staff, if true
- clean/comfortable clinic
- doctor/team names, if appropriate

## Best moments to ask

Ask for reviews after:
- happy dental/spay/neuter visits
- successful urgent visits with clear relief
- new puppy/kitten appointments
- long-term client visits
- cases where the client verbally praises staff
- visits where client says pricing was fair or communication was clear

Avoid asking after:
- billing disputes
- long waits
- complex or poor outcomes
- surprise cost concerns
- unhappy urgent cases
- medication/refill frustration

## Review ask message

Use after a good visit:

```
Hi [First Name], thank you for trusting Angel Pet Hospital with [Pet Name]. If you had a good experience with our team, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It helps other local pet owners find reliable care in Edmonds.

[Google review link]
```

## Negative review response framework

For negative reviews:

1. Acknowledge concern.
2. Do not argue publicly.
3. Mention care, safety, or communication standards.
4. Invite direct contact.
5. Keep it concise.

### Pricing complaint template

```
Thank you for sharing your feedback. We’re sorry the cost did not feel clearly explained. Our goal is to provide transparent estimates before treatment whenever possible, and we’d appreciate the chance to review your visit and answer any billing questions. Please call our team at (425) 673-0700 so we can better understand what happened.
```

### Cleanliness complaint template

```
We’re sorry to hear this feedback. Cleanliness and patient safety are very important to us, and we take concerns like this seriously. Please call us at (425) 673-0700 so our management team can learn more about your visit and address it properly.
```

### Medication/refill complaint template

```
We’re sorry for the frustration. Medication and refill decisions can depend on medical history, exam timing, and patient safety. We’d appreciate the chance to review your case and explain the next step clearly. Please call us at (425) 673-0700.
```

## Review monitoring cadence

Weekly:
- respond to all new reviews
- flag low-star reviews
- identify positive themes worth using in ads/website copy

Monthly:
- record rating and review count
- count new 5-star reviews
- count negative reviews
- track response rate
- summarize client sentiment themes

## Client-facing KPI

Report monthly:
- rating
- total reviews
- new reviews this month
- number of responses posted
- top positive themes
- top negative themes
- next action plan
