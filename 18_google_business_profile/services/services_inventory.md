# GBP Services Inventory And Cleanup Plan

Initial source: Google Business Profile Services screenshot reviewed on 2026-06-30.

## Objective

Clean the GBP services list so it supports:

- accurate user expectations
- local SEO relevance
- Google Ads alignment
- website service-page alignment
- fewer low-quality calls

## Current issue

The GBP service list appears cluttered with duplicates, generic items, and potentially risky claims.

Examples flagged:
- 24/7 Emergency Services
- Home Services
- Pet Treatment
- Surgery Procedure
- Surgical Procedure
- Veterinary Services For Dogs
- Animal Poison Control
- Care Credit
- Normal Checkup
- Routine Checkups
- Same Day Appointments
- TPLO Surgery

## Recommended service naming framework

Use clear service names that match what real pet owners search and what the clinic actually provides.

### Core services to keep or add if true

- Wellness Exams
- Sick Pet Visits
- Urgent Vet Care During Business Hours
- Vaccinations
- Pet Dental Care
- Dental Cleanings
- Spay and Neuter
- Veterinary Surgery
- Diagnostic Testing
- In-House Lab
- X-Rays / Radiology
- Senior Pet Care
- Puppy and Kitten Care
- Microchipping
- Pain Management
- Wound Care

### Confirm before keeping

These should not remain unless the clinic confirms they are accurate and operationally supported:

- 24/7 Emergency Services
- Home Services
- Animal Poison Control
- Same Day Appointments
- TPLO Surgery
- Exotic Vet Care
- Mobile Veterinarian / House Calls

### Remove or replace

| Current / risky item | Recommended action | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 24/7 Emergency Services | Replace with Urgent Vet Care During Business Hours if true | Avoid false 24/7 expectation |
| Home Services | Remove unless clinic provides home visits | Avoid mobile-vet lead mismatch |
| Animal Poison Control | Remove unless truly provided | Usually not a clinic service |
| Care Credit | Move to payment/payment options messaging, not services | Financing is not a veterinary service |
| Pet Treatment | Remove or replace with Sick Pet Visits | Too generic |
| Normal Checkup | Replace with Wellness Exams | Better search/user language |
| Routine Checkups | Merge into Wellness Exams | Duplicate |
| Surgery Procedure | Merge into Veterinary Surgery | Duplicate/awkward |
| Surgical Procedure | Merge into Veterinary Surgery | Duplicate/awkward |
| Veterinary Services For Dogs | Replace with Dog Veterinary Care if useful | Awkward wording |
| Same Day Appointments | Keep only if reliably true | High operational risk |
| TPLO Surgery | Keep only if confirmed | Advanced service claim risk |

## Service descriptions guidance

Each major service should have a short, clear description when GBP allows it.

Example:

### Pet Dental Care
Dental exams and cleanings for dogs and cats, with estimates provided after examination. Tooth extractions and additional care may affect final cost.

### Spay and Neuter
Spay and neuter procedures for eligible dogs and cats. Final estimate depends on species, sex, weight, and examination findings.

### Urgent Vet Care During Business Hours
Care for urgent pet concerns during business hours. Call ahead so our team can guide the best next step.

## Do-not-claim rules

Do not claim:
- 24/7 care unless true
- home visits unless true
- guaranteed same-day appointments unless true
- exotic care unless true
- advanced orthopedic surgery unless true
- free or low-cost clinic positioning unless intentional

## Approval needed from clinic

Before editing services, confirm:

1. Do they offer urgent care during all posted hours?
2. Do they offer 24/7 emergency care? Likely no.
3. Do they offer home visits?
4. Do they treat exotics?
5. Do they perform TPLO surgery?
6. Are same-day appointments reliably available?
7. Which dental, spay, neuter, surgery, diagnostic, and wellness services should be actively promoted?
