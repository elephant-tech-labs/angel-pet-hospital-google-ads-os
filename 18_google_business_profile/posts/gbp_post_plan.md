# Google Business Profile Post Plan

Initial audit date: 2026-06-30

## Current issue

Existing GBP posts appeared outdated, with examples published around 3 years ago.

This makes the profile look inactive and misses opportunities to support:
- current promotions
- service awareness
- new patient conversion
- local trust
- website clicks

## Posting cadence

Recommended:
- 1 post per week for the first 8 weeks
- then 2 posts per month minimum

## First 8 posts

1. Dental, Spay & Neuter Offers Available
2. New Patients Welcome
3. Urgent Vet Care During Business Hours
4. Pet Dental Care Reminder
5. Meet the Team / Clinic Photo
6. Puppy & Kitten Wellness Visits
7. Senior Pet Care Reminder
8. Vaccination / Wellness Reminder

## Do not include in GBP post

- Full price table
- Guaranteed final price language
- 24/7 wording
- Aggressive coupon-style wording
- Fake urgency without confirmed expiration date

## Monthly process

Each month:
1. Review upcoming offers/services to promote.
2. Draft 4 posts.
3. Confirm claims with clinic.
4. Publish weekly.
5. Track clicks and calls where possible.
6. Record posts in this folder.
